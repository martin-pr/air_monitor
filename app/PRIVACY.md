# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2, 2026

Air Monitor is an open-source Android home-screen widget for displaying
sensor readings from an ESP32-based air monitor.

The app passively scans for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisements
from the device and displays the latest sensor readings. It does not
connect to, pair with, or transmit data to the device.

## Information Collection

Air Monitor does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal
information.

The app does not use: - Analytics - Advertising - User accounts -
Tracking technologies - Backend servers - Firebase - Crashlytics - Cloud
services operated by the developer

## Local Operation

Any data shown by the app is received locally on the user's device from
nearby BLE advertisements and is used only to display air-monitor
readings.

## Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect
information related to app download, installation, updates, crash
reporting, and Play Store usage under Google's own privacy policies. The
developer may only receive aggregated information made available through
Google Play Console.
